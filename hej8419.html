Building Better Study Habits

Help Jisu build better study habits while learning six useful English expressions.

Step 1 of 4 25%

Meet Jisu 👋

Read about Jisu. What problems does she have with her study habits?

Jisu wants to study hard. She even tries to learn new things during break time. However, she sometimes studies for too long without resting.

She often checks messages on her phone while studying, and she does not always know how much homework she has completed.

Jisu also goes to bed late when she has a lot of homework. When something is difficult, she sometimes gives up on her study plan.

Can you help Jisu build better study habits?

1. Learn the Expressions

Click each expression to check its meaning and example.

keep track of
~을 계속 확인하다 / 기록하며 파악하다
Example: I use a planner to keep track of my homework.
take a break
휴식을 취하다
Example: You should take a break after studying for an hour.
stay away from
~을 멀리하다 / 피하다
Example: Try to stay away from your phone while studying.
get enough sleep
충분한 수면을 취하다
Example: Students need to get enough sleep every night.
deal with
~을 처리하다 / 대처하다
Example: I make a plan to deal with difficult homework.
stick to
~을 계속 지키다 / 고수하다
Example: Try to stick to your study schedule.
